# Attribution and rights

This is student work from the Hardware for Neural Networks lab at TU Darmstadt, summer semester 2026. It does not imply any endorsement by the university.

The course provided the framework, the model, the Jinja2 templates, the supporting RTL and the UART board test flow. `FC1.sv` and `FC2.sv` are generated from those templates. The README says which parts are mine. Codex reworked the UART wrapper and wrote its testbenches, and AI tools helped with the local check scripts and the documentation.

Course handouts and the full course datasets are not redistributed here. The repository keeps 16 samples as a simulation fixture.

No open-source license is granted. The repository mixes my own work with course material, so attribution and existing rights stay with their respective authors and owners. Tool, board and institution names belong to their owners.
